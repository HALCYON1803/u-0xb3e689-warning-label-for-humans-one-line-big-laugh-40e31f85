one single-line safety warning label that should come printed on every
human being, at most 160 characters.

## The line

> WARNING: Do not negotiate with this human on an empty stomach. It will trade its car for a sandwich and call it a win.

## Why this line

Dry safety-label voice ("WARNING: Do not...") wrapped around one very
true human defect: hunger destroys judgement. The laugh lands in the
last few words — the trade is bad, and the human is proud of it anyway.
One idea, one turn, 120 characters.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line).
